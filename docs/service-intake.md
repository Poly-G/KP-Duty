# Versioned service intake

Existing projects retain template v1. New projects bind to v2, with fuller service questions, common accurate business facts, contacts, agreed scope and dependencies. Website intake asks about the customer path, proof, existing brand/assets, domain and enquiry destination without requiring finished copy. Branding, Less Office, Care and Sent have distinct fields; Sent includes audience/signals, baseline, access and handoff ownership.

Website projects with branding in their agreed scope expose required brand context/constraints. Existing-brand website projects do not require that branch. Optional notes are never a submission gate. Partial drafts can be saved and resumed; “Not sure” captures unresolved details for review rather than inventing facts. Company name prefills a draft fact field but does not mark anything submitted or accepted.

Submission, staff review and admin delivery start remain separate. Database validation enforces the bound template and conditional questions even for direct API calls. Changed answers reset submission/review and retain history. Template identity cannot be changed in place. This does not define unresolved commercial terms, automatically accept scope changes, certify brand direction/copy production gates, or replace client authentication.
